We Walk With You

I ardently believe in our capacity as human beings to experience healing. I see my role as a Licensed Clinical Professional Counselor to partner and walk with my clients while they tap into their resiliency to make positive changes in their lives. Counseling is a unique relationship that provides a safe space for the exploration of thoughts, feelings, behaviors, values, beliefs, relationships, challenges and solutions. I believe that you are the expert on you and that I can work with you to fulfill your counseling goal(s).

I work with individuals (older teens and adults) and couples. I have a varied clinical background including: trauma, compassion fatigue, couples work & intimate relationships, stress reduction, grief, anxiety, depression and psychometry. I am a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP). Promoting professional resiliency is a passion of mine. As a Certified Compassion Fatigue Professional (CCFP), I facilitate workshops and groups focused on eliminating symptoms of compassion fatigue and bolstering resiliency.

The counseling theories that inform my work are: Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy, Person-Centered Theory, Mindfulness, Forward Facing Trauma Therapy as well as Choice Theory. I use Gottman Method when working with couples. I would be happy to explain my techniques more in depth with you and explore how we can use them to best meet your unique needs.

I hold a doctorate (PhD) in Counselor Education and Supervision and a masters’ degree (MA) in Counselor Education: Clinical Mental Health Counseling from the University of Montana (UM). I also teach at UM as an adjunct professor. When not engaging in work, I can often be found along a mountain trail with my beloved husband, son and dog.
