We Walk With You

At NRC, we ardently believe in our capacity as human beings to experience healing. We are a group practice that provides compassionate and respectful mental health counseling and related services to promote wellness for the greater Missoula community. While honoring diversity, individual differences, and strengths, Northern Rockies Counseling’s (NRC) mission is to assist clients, and community members intentionally explore their full potential across the broad spectrum of the human experience and to walk with others as they endeavor to align with their authentic selves. This can often be accomplished within safe and non-judgmental relationships in which the therapeutic alliance is intentionally nurtured. We foster collaboration and feedback to help clients achieve their established goals.

NRC clinicians come from varied identities, educational and professional backgrounds and interests, worldviews, and lived experiences. Yet our united goal is to walk with our clients as they seek to embody their capacity using relationship-based, trauma-informed, and strength-based counseling approaches. Services include individual, couples, family and group counseling as well as workshops and professional trainings. We are accepting new referrals for individual, couples, and family counseling. As a Team we are skilled at providing trauma-informed and inclusive approaches across lifespans (age 8+) for clients navigating life transitions, relationship conflicts, anxiety, depression, complex trauma, chronic illness, identity-related issues, disability, LGBTQIA+ specific concerns, and body image challenges.

As part of our commitment to enhance the field of professional counseling, we have the distinct honor of supervising license-eligible clinicians at our practice. The Clinical Supervisor and owner of NRC is Julie Ballew, PhD, LCPC. If you are receiving counseling from a license-eligible counselor (PCLC), please know that she oversees all their clinical work. We meet regularly to review counseling sessions, and she examines all session notes. Each supervisee has their own distinct experiences and areas of expertise. It is sort of like getting two counselors in one! All billing of license-eligible counselors is done under Dr. Ballew’s name and license; this is standard practice until counselors have completed the thousands of clinical hours needed for licensure. If you have questions about how clinical supervision works, please do not hesitate to ask your counselor or Dr. Ballew.

We offer both in-person and telehealth options. Additionally, we would welcome opportunities to collaborate with you and your organization through psychoeducational groups/workshops and group counseling offerings. We look forward to partnering with you to help serve the greater Missoula Community!
